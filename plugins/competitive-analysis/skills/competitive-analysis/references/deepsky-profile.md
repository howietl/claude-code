### Product Description

DeepSky is an AI "superagent" for business professionals, focused on deep research and analysis. Unlike surface-level AI summaries, DeepSky conducts thorough research, reasons deeply, and delivers comprehensive analysis in multiple formats (reports, slides, documents, websites). The company was acquired by Airtable in October 2025 in their largest acquisition to date.

### Target Market

**Primary:**
- Startup founders and executives
- Finance professionals and investment firms
- Strategic planners and consultants

**Secondary:**
- Product managers
- Marketing strategists
- SMB operators

## SWOT Analysis

### Strengths

1. **Deep Research Quality** - Beyond surface-level summaries
2. **Finance Specialization** - Domain expertise baked in
3. **Multi-Format Delivery** - Reports, slides, docs, websites
4. **Technical Innovation** - Million-token context, high accuracy
5. **Airtable Backing** - Resources and distribution post-acquisition
6. **"Entrepreneur of One" Vision** - Compelling positioning

### Weaknesses

1. **Not Code-Focused** - Research agent, not development tool
2. **Narrow Use Case** - Business research, not general coding
3. **Acquisition Integration Risk** - May lose focus under Airtable
4. **Limited Runs at Pro Tier** - 200/month may feel constrained
5. **New to Coding** - Coding features still in development

### Opportunities

1. **Airtable Distribution** - Access to Airtable's enterprise customer base
2. **Coding Expansion** - Adding actualization/coding capabilities
3. **Enterprise Research** - Due diligence, M&A, strategy teams
4. **Finance Vertical Dominance** - Own the finance research niche

### Threats

1. **Model Provider Agents** - OpenAI, Anthropic building research agents
2. **Perplexity** - Strong in research/search
3. **Post-Acquisition Focus** - May be absorbed into Airtable product
4. **Manus Competition** - General-purpose agents can do research

## Airtable Acquisition Analysis

### Why Airtable Acquired DeepSky

1. **AI Strategy** - Airtable needs AI capabilities to stay competitive
2. **CTO Hire** - David Azose (ex-OpenAI) as new CTO with acquisition
3. **Product Integration** - DeepSky research in Airtable workflows
4. **Enterprise AI** - Airtable serves enterprise, needs AI features
5. **Talent Acquisition** - Strong AI/finance team

### Implications

**Positive:**
- More resources for development
- Enterprise distribution channel
- Integration with Airtable data workflows
- Credibility boost

**Risks:**
- Loss of independent focus
- Product absorbed vs. standalone
- Team retention challenges
- Strategic pivots

### Watch Points

- Will DeepSky remain standalone product?
- How deep will Airtable integration go?
- Will finance specialization continue or generalize?
- Team retention post-acquisition
