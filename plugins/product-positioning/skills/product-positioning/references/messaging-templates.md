# Messaging Templates and Examples

## Value Proposition Templates

### Template 1: Problem-Solution-Outcome

> [Target audience] struggle with [problem]. [Product] [solution approach] so they can [desired outcome].

**Example**:
> Engineering teams struggle with slow, unreliable deployments. Acme Deploy automates your entire CI/CD pipeline so they can ship code 10x faster with zero downtime.

### Template 2: Comparison-Based

> Unlike [alternative/status quo], [Product] [key differentiator], which means [benefit].

**Example**:
> Unlike traditional spreadsheet-based planning, Acme Planning provides real-time scenario modeling, which means you can respond to market changes in hours instead of weeks.

### Template 3: Outcome-First

> [Desired outcome] without [pain point]. [Product] [how it works].

**Example**:
> Enterprise-grade security without the enterprise complexity. Acme Security deploys in 15 minutes and protects your entire cloud infrastructure automatically.

### Template 4: For-Who-Unlike

> For [target customer] who need [requirement], [Product] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternatives], [Product] [differentiation].

**Example**:
> For data teams who need to democratize analytics, Acme Analytics is a self-service BI platform that enables anyone to build dashboards without SQL. Unlike traditional BI tools, Acme uses natural language queries so business users can get answers in seconds.

## Headline Formulas

### Benefit-Driven Headlines

- [Verb] + [outcome] + in [timeframe]
  - "Close deals 40% faster in your first month"

- [Outcome] without [pain]
  - "Enterprise security without enterprise complexity"

- The [adjective] way to [desired action]
  - "The fastest way to deploy machine learning models"

### Problem-Agitation Headlines

- Stop [frustrating activity]. Start [desired activity].
  - "Stop chasing down data. Start making decisions."

- [Pain point] is costing you [quantified impact]
  - "Manual reporting is costing you 20 hours per week"

### Curiosity Headlines

- How [customer type] [achieve outcome]
  - "How high-growth startups scale customer support without scaling headcount"

- Why [surprising insight]
  - "Why the fastest-growing companies don't use spreadsheets for planning"

## Social Proof Formats

### Customer Quote Templates

**Results-focused**:
> "[Product] helped us [specific outcome] in [timeframe]. We've seen [metric improvement] since implementing." — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

**Before/After**:
> "Before [Product], we were [pain point]. Now, [improved state]. It's been a game-changer for our team." — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

**Recommendation**:
> "If you're struggling with [problem], [Product] is a no-brainer. We evaluated [alternatives] and nothing came close." — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

### Case Study Snippet Format

**[Customer Name]**: [One-line description of customer]
- **Challenge**: [Brief problem statement]
- **Solution**: [How they used the product]
- **Results**: [Key metrics/outcomes]

## Boilerplate Templates

### Company Boilerplate (Press Release)

> [Company Name] is a [category] company that helps [target customers] [achieve outcome]. Founded in [year], [Company Name] [brief differentiator or proof point]. The company is backed by [investors] and serves customers including [notable customers]. For more information, visit [website].

**Example**:
> Acme Analytics is a business intelligence company that helps data teams democratize analytics across their organization. Founded in 2020, Acme's natural language interface enables anyone to build dashboards without writing SQL. The company is backed by Sequoia and Greylock and serves customers including Stripe, Notion, and Figma. For more information, visit acmeanalytics.com.

### Product Boilerplate

> [Product Name] is a [category] that [key capability]. [Key differentiator]. [Proof point: customer count, metric, or award].

**Example**:
> Acme Deploy is a CI/CD platform that automates software deployments from code commit to production. Unlike traditional DevOps tools, Acme requires zero configuration and deploys in minutes. Trusted by over 5,000 engineering teams worldwide.
