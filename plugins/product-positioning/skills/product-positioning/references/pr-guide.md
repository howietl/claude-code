# Press Release and PR Guide

## When to Issue a Press Release

### High-Priority Announcements
- **Funding rounds**: Series A and beyond (Seed sometimes, depending on amount and investors)
- **Major product launches**: New products or significant platform updates
- **Strategic acquisitions**: Acquiring or being acquired
- **Major partnerships**: Enterprise partnerships, strategic integrations
- **Significant milestones**: Major customer wins, revenue milestones, user growth

### Medium-Priority Announcements
- **Executive hires**: C-suite and VP-level additions
- **Awards and recognition**: Industry awards, analyst recognition
- **Major customer wins**: Especially well-known logos (with permission)
- **New market entry**: Geographic expansion, new verticals

### Skip the Press Release
- Minor feature updates
- Small partnerships
- Routine hires
- Internal milestones

**Rule of thumb**: If it's not genuinely newsworthy to someone outside your company, it's not press release material.

## Press Release Structure

### 1. Headline
- Clear and specific—state the news directly
- Include company name
- 10-12 words maximum
- Avoid superlatives ("revolutionary", "groundbreaking")

**Good**: "Acme Analytics Raises $50M Series B to Expand AI-Powered Business Intelligence"
**Bad**: "Revolutionary Analytics Startup Closes Massive Funding Round"

### 2. Subheadline
- Adds important context not in headline
- Often includes a key proof point
- 15-20 words

**Example**: "Funding led by Sequoia Capital will accelerate product development and international expansion"

### 3. Dateline
- City, State/Country — Date

**Example**: "SAN FRANCISCO — January 15, 2025"

### 4. Lead Paragraph
- Who, what, when, where, why in 2-3 sentences
- Most important information first
- Should stand alone as a summary

**Example**:
> Acme Analytics, a business intelligence platform that enables natural language data queries, today announced it has raised $50 million in Series B funding. The round was led by Sequoia Capital with participation from existing investors Greylock and Index Ventures. The company will use the funds to expand its AI capabilities and enter the European market.

### 5. First Quote
- From founder, CEO, or most senior relevant executive
- Provides perspective on significance
- Should sound human, not corporate

**Good**: "Data teams have been stuck writing SQL for 30 years. We're finally changing that. This funding lets us bring self-service analytics to every business user, not just data scientists."
**Bad**: "We are thrilled to announce this strategic investment that validates our vision and positions us for continued growth."

### 6. Body Paragraphs
- Supporting details and context
- Product/company background
- Relevant metrics and proof points
- Market context

**Include**:
- Current customer count or growth rate
- Key product capabilities
- Relevant industry trends
- How this announcement fits the company's trajectory

### 7. Second Quote
- From investor, partner, or customer
- Provides external validation
- Different perspective than first quote

**From Investor**:
> "Acme has built something rare—a product that genuinely delights users while delivering enterprise-grade capabilities," said [Partner Name], Partner at Sequoia Capital. "Their customer retention and expansion metrics are among the best we've seen in the BI space."

**From Customer**:
> "Acme changed how our entire company works with data," said [Customer Name], VP of Analytics at [Company]. "What used to take our data team days now takes anyone on the team minutes."

### 8. Boilerplate
- Standard company description (2-3 sentences)
- Consistent across all releases
- Include founding year, category, key differentiator

### 9. Contact Information
- PR/media contact name
- Email and phone
- Company website

## Writing Style Guidelines

### Do
- Lead with the most newsworthy element
- Use active voice
- Include specific numbers and metrics
- Attribute claims to named individuals
- Write in third person
- Keep sentences short and clear
- Target 400-600 words total

### Don't
- Use promotional adjectives (innovative, revolutionary, cutting-edge)
- Bury the news in paragraph three
- Use jargon without explanation
- Make unsubstantiated claims
- Include calls-to-action or sales language
- Exceed 800 words

### Quote Writing Tips

**Make quotes sound human**:
- Include contractions
- Use first person
- Express genuine emotion or conviction
- Avoid corporate-speak

**Quote purposes**:
- First quote: Significance and vision
- Second quote: External validation
- Third quote (if needed): Technical or product detail

## Funding Announcement Template

```
[HEADLINE: Company Name Raises $[Amount] [Round] to [Purpose]]

[SUBHEADLINE: Led by [Lead Investor], funding will [key use of funds]]

[CITY] — [DATE] — [Company Name], [brief descriptor], today announced it has raised $[amount] in [Series X] funding. The round was led by [lead investor] with participation from [other investors]. [One sentence on use of funds or significance].

[Company Name] [brief background—what the company does and key traction]. Since [last milestone/founding], the company has [growth metrics]. The new funding will be used to [specific plans].

"[Quote from CEO about significance, market opportunity, and vision]," said [CEO Name], CEO of [Company Name].

[Paragraph on product and market context]

[Paragraph on specific plans for the funding]

"[Quote from lead investor about why they invested]," said [Investor Name], [Title] at [Firm Name].

[Optional: customer quote or additional context]

About [Company Name]
[Boilerplate]

[Name]
```

## Distribution Strategy

### Wire Services
- **PR Newswire**: Broadest distribution, best for major announcements
- **Business Wire**: Strong financial and business media reach
- **GlobeNewswire**: Cost-effective option
- **eReleases**: Good for startups and smaller announcements

### Direct Outreach
- Pitch relevant journalists directly before/after wire
- Personalize the angle for each outlet
- Offer exclusive interviews or data

### Owned Channels
- Company blog post (more detailed than press release)
- Email to customers and subscribers
- Social media (LinkedIn, Twitter/X)
- Community channels (Slack, Discord)

### Timing
- Tuesday-Thursday, early morning (7-9am local time)
- Avoid holidays and major news events
- Embargo for coordinated coverage (trusted journalists only)

## Measurement

Track these metrics:
- **Pickup**: Number of publications that covered
- **Quality**: Tier of publications (top-tier, trade, blog)
- **Message pull-through**: Did coverage include key messages?
- **Traffic**: Website visits from coverage
- **Backlinks**: SEO value from coverage
- **Social amplification**: Shares and mentions

## Building Media Relationships

### Before You Need Coverage
- Follow and engage with relevant journalists
- Share their work (without expecting anything)
- Be a helpful source for industry trends
- Offer data and insights proactively

### When Pitching
- Research the journalist's beat and recent work
- Personalize the pitch—explain why it's relevant to them
- Keep pitches short (3-4 sentences max)
- Offer something exclusive if possible
- Respect their time and deadlines

### After Coverage
- Thank journalists (briefly, not effusively)
- Share the coverage on your channels
- Stay in touch for future opportunities
