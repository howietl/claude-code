# Press Release Template with Annotations

## Annotated Template

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HEADLINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[Company Name] [Action Verb] [Key News] to [Benefit/Outcome]

► Keep to 10-12 words maximum
► Include company name
► Lead with the news, not the company
► Use active verbs (Raises, Launches, Acquires, Partners)
► Avoid promotional adjectives (revolutionary, groundbreaking)

Example: "Acme Analytics Raises $50M Series B to Democratize Business Intelligence"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SUBHEADLINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[Key Supporting Detail or Proof Point]

► 15-20 words
► Adds context not in headline
► Often includes: lead investor, growth metric, or key customer

Example: "AI-powered platform has tripled revenue year-over-year, now serves 2,000+ companies"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DATELINE + LEAD PARAGRAPH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[CITY] — [Month Day, Year] — [Company Name], [one-line descriptor], today
announced [the news]. [Key supporting fact]. [Why it matters or what's next].

► City should be company headquarters
► First sentence: who + what + when
► Second sentence: supporting fact or context
► Third sentence: significance or future direction
► Should stand alone as a complete summary

Example:
"SAN FRANCISCO — January 15, 2025 — Acme Analytics, the business intelligence
platform that enables natural language data queries, today announced it has
raised $50 million in Series B funding. The round was led by Sequoia Capital
with participation from existing investors Greylock and Index Ventures. The
funding will accelerate Acme's AI capabilities and expansion into Europe."

[Market problem or opportunity]. [How company/product addresses it]. [Relevant

► Explain why this news matters
► Include relevant market context

► Explain why this matters
► Should sound human, not corporate

► What specifically will happen next
► More detailed information for interested readers
► Can include bullet points for features/plans
```

## Complete Example: Funding Announcement

---

**Acme Analytics Raises $50M Series B to Democratize Business Intelligence**

*AI-powered platform has tripled revenue year-over-year, now serves 2,000+ companies*

SAN FRANCISCO — January 15, 2025 — Acme Analytics, the business intelligence platform that enables natural language data queries, today announced it has raised $50 million in Series B funding. The round was led by Sequoia Capital with participation from existing investors Greylock and Index Ventures. The funding will accelerate Acme's AI capabilities and expansion into Europe.

Data teams spend an average of 40% of their time writing SQL queries and building reports, leaving little time for strategic analysis. Acme's natural language interface allows anyone in an organization to query data and build dashboards without technical skills. Since launching two years ago, the platform has processed over 10 million queries for customers including Stripe, Notion, and Figma.

"We started Acme because we believed everyone should be able to work with data, not just analysts who know SQL," said Jane Chen, CEO of Acme Analytics. "This funding lets us take that mission global. We're investing heavily in AI to make data analysis as easy as asking a question."

The company will use the funding to double its engineering team, expand AI capabilities including GPT-4 integration, and open its first European office in London. Acme has grown from 50 to 200 employees over the past year and plans to reach 400 by year end.

"Acme has built the interface for data that we've been waiting for," said Michael Brown, Partner at Sequoia Capital. "Their customer expansion metrics are remarkable—over 150% net revenue retention—because once teams try it, it spreads across the organization. We see Acme becoming the default way people interact with data."

Acme was recently named to the Forbes Cloud 100 and recognized as a Gartner Cool Vendor in Analytics. The company's customer base includes 15% of the Fortune 500, with particularly strong adoption in financial services and technology sectors.

**About Acme Analytics**

Acme Analytics is a business intelligence company that enables anyone to analyze data using natural language. Founded in 2022, Acme's AI-powered platform serves over 2,000 companies including Stripe, Notion, and Figma. The company is backed by Sequoia Capital, Greylock, and Index Ventures. For more information, visit acmeanalytics.com.

## Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Headline is under 12 words and includes company name
- [ ] Lead paragraph answers who, what, when, where, why
- [ ] Quotes sound human, not corporate
- [ ] All claims are substantiated with data or attribution
- [ ] No promotional adjectives (revolutionary, groundbreaking, etc.)
- [ ] Total length is 400-600 words
- [ ] Boilerplate is current and accurate
- [ ] Contact info is correct and monitored
- [ ] Any named customers/partners have approved mention
- [ ] Legal has reviewed if required
